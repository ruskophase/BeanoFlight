USB camera recording window

Elapsed seconds after feeding first starts, including pauses. +10 to +40 requests 30 seconds. The window must fit within the planned feeding duration; END or STOP can cut it short. Actual acknowledgements and file paths are recorded separately.

GPIO33 trigger settings from Waveform Generator

External recording enables this output for the recording window, then disables it. Timing is configured before paired-camera startup.
