What these values mean

99% capture probability means a targeted bean has a 99% chance of reaching Selected and a 1% chance of continuing into Pass-through. A seeded simulation draws one outcome per bean. It does not force exactly one miss per hundred. Intentionally passed beans stay in Pass-through.

Expected profiles use fractional counts from this probability. Simulated outcomes remain synthetic. Neither changes recorded mass nor confirms a physical capture. This setting does not change real machine controls or the capture model of an existing job.

The mean is specific to the material described above. New simulated runs use the intended processing mass to estimate their particle count, rounded to the nearest integer. Receiving records net mass and source details; particle count and other bean properties remain unknown at that stage. A smaller test count can be entered explicitly when starting a simulation.
